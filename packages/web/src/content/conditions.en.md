## The service

OhMyWind is a free tool to help prepare a sailing passage: it gathers public wind,
wave, tide and current forecasts, and estimates the duration and difficulty of a trip.

These terms apply in the same way to every way of reaching the service: the website
[ohmywind.fr](https://ohmywind.fr), the Android app and the connector for AI assistants
(MCP server at `mcp.ohmywind.fr`). Using any of them means accepting these terms.

## What the service is not

OhMyWind is **neither a navigation instrument nor an official weather bulletin**. It
does not replace official marine weather bulletins (Météo-France, the coastguard or the
national weather service for the area), up-to-date nautical charts, or the skipper's
judgement.

You alone remain responsible for your navigation decisions, for preparing your boat
and for the safety of your crew. Check the official bulletins before every departure.

## No warranty

unavailable. Durations and difficulty ratings are estimates computed for typical
boats, not for yours. Tidal streams are read from atlases whose resolution can be too
coarse in narrow passes.
